NQ-open retrieval demo

Ask a factual question. The system searches 2.0M Wikipedia passages (hybrid dense + BM25), then a fine-tuned cross-encoder reranks the top 50. Words from your question are highlighted.

Try: when did the eiffel tower openwho sang hotel californiahow many players on a rugby team
Corpus: the Wikipedia pages behind 100k Natural Questions, not all of Wikipedia. On held-out questions the answer is in the top 10 for 91% of questions. Reranker: NezarIn/nq-open-minilm-reranker.
